A form submission is an email and a claim. This playbook turns it into an account brief, a qualification decision, and an assigned next step, with a person on everything uncertain.

The full method is below. The judgment at its center is one split. Fit and intent are scored apart, so enthusiasm cannot fake fit.

A buyer, on where the real deals come from

How it works

01Capture the claim

A form submission is an email and a claim of interest. Store both verbatim, with where the person arrived from.

Treat the claim as a claim. A download alone is not buying intent, and a router that assumes otherwise sells to students and spams recruiters.

02Enrich with evidence

Company, size, role, from public sources, with every claim carrying where it came from. Unknown stays unknown, never guessed.

Spend follows use. Enrichment runs on sign-ups something downstream will actually read, capped per day.

03Write the account brief

One screen. What we verified, what we could not, with sources. The brief serves the router and the human reviewer the same, so a person can audit any decision in a minute.

04Score fit and intent apart

Enthusiasm cannot fake fit. Fit scores who they are. Intent scores what they did. Kept separate, a crawler visiting twenty-five times is intent without fit, and a perfect-fit visitor on day one is fit without intent. Different numbers, different plays.

Watch for the single combined score. It routes the enthusiastic wrong-fit visitor above the quiet right one.

05Route by band

Qualified means fit and intent both clear the bands you set, and the sign-up gets a named owner with a dated next step. Right fit without readiness routes to nurture. No fit routes to a polite, useful response and nothing more.

Students, recruiters, and bots deserve the resource they asked for. They never deserve a sales sequence.

06Drop uncertainty to a person

Conflicting evidence, borderline bands, and duplicates drop to a review queue with the reasons attached. Duplicates get matched against existing accounts before anything new is created.

Watch for the router that never asks for help. A queue that is always empty means the rules are guessing.

07Evaluate on the four personas

The kit ships four sample sign-ups. A potential customer, a recruiter, a student, and someone whose intent is unknown. Run them before any real submission and compare against the answer key.

Supervised first. The router earns unattended routing on clean passes, and the no-fit lane earns it last.

The form told you someone came. The brief tells you who, with receipts.
