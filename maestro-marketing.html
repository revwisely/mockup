// The Problem

What marketing leaders are
actually dealing with

The tools were bought one at a time and connected by whoever was there that year.

Records disagree across four systems, so the number in the board deck gets assembled by hand. Leads arrive and sit, because follow-up depends on somebody noticing. Buying signals fire in one tool and never reach the person who could act on them. Every campaign starts by cleaning up before it can start.

The marketing agents

Demand and audience. Demand creation, audience segmentation, and lead qualification scored against a 100-point model covering title fit, company fit, signal strength, and engagement.
