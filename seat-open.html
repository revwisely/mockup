// Fair questions

What hiring leaders ask first

Why not wait for the hire?
The wait runs past the search. A new hire still has to learn your stack and build the system before it produces. Starting now means they start ahead.
Does this replace the role?
No. Keep the search running. Everything is built for the person who fills the seat to run and own.
Aren’t we paying twice once the hire starts?
What you pay for is a running system in your stack. It keeps producing after the salary starts, and your hire spends their first months running and extending it.
What if we hire mid-build?
They join the build and own it from their first day.
How much of our time does this take?
One call to pick the first system, then a short review each week. Access to your CRM and tools is set up in week one.
