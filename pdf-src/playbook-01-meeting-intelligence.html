The method

How It Works

Seven steps. The runnable versions of every rubric here ship as the markdown files in this kit, ready to paste into your model.

01Capture the call

Any recorder that produces a transcript works. Raw speaker-labeled transcripts produce the best extractions. Notetaker summaries still work, and the workflow lowers its confidence scores to match, because secondhand notes are already an interpretation.

02Extract with receipts

No quote, no item. The workflow extracts what was said. It never invents. Every item carries the verbatim words that created it, so anyone can audit any entry back to the source. Anything the meeting explicitly deferred becomes a follow-up, never a task.

Watch for the item you cannot quote. Cut it. A phantom commitment costs more than a missed one.

03Route what was said

Meetings wander. Two facts get judged separately per item. Was it agreed, and is it in scope. The route follows from the pair.

RouteThe tellWhat happens
Committed, in scopeAn owner and a yes. "Yes, ship it."Work item this cycle
Committed, out of scopeAgreed in the room, but scope does not cover itProceeds, always with a scope note
ExploredMusing. No owner, no dateParking lot, no task
Non-goal reversalAlready scoped out, came backFlagged; a person re-decides

The tell is an owner and a yes. Enthusiasm alone is explored. The parking lot is expansion signal, and promotion out of it is a human act, always.

04Fork by owner

Items owned by your side are candidates for execution. Items owned by the other side become waiting-on entries and are never executed. That single rule lets tomorrow's brief say "waiting on Dana since the 14th."

05Apply the tier rules

Rules decide the tier. The stricter rule always wins. Reversible, scoped, testable work is eligible to run unattended. Large items wait. Low-confidence items wait. And anything a person outside your team will see, in their words or yours, waits for a person on your side. Always, regardless of size.

Watch for the tier assigned by vibes. If you cannot name the rule that decided it, the rule layer is missing.

06File and brief

Approved output lands where your team already works. Record updates drafted into the CRM. Tasks with an owner, a size, and the source quote. A channel brief a teammate reads in a minute. The front end is a door, and you should name your own. Slack teams get a Slack brief, Microsoft teams get Teams.

07Graduate autonomy

Supervised first. Graduation is measured, never assumed. A person reviews every extraction at the start, scoring five failure modes. Invented items, missed asks, wrong owners, wrong routes, unfaithful quotes. The workflow writes on its own only after consecutive clean passes, and only for the tiers the rules allow. Trust is a property you measure, with a date attached.

The Shape of One Extracted Item

{
  "title": "Send pricing summary to their CFO",
  "owner": "us",
  "estimate": "S",
  "commitment": "committed",
  "scope": "in",
  "tier_proposed": "review",
  "source_quote": "Can you get the pricing summary over to our CFO by Friday?"
}

The scales, defined

  • Estimate. S = under an hour. M = fits in a day. L = more than a day, or cannot honestly be estimated. Torn between two, pick the larger.
  • Confidence. high = explicit and unambiguous. medium = clear ask, inferred criteria. low = a reasonable reader would ask "did they actually agree to that?" Notes-sourced input drops one level.

In this kit

The Runnable Files

The markdown files in this kit are the working versions. Markdown pastes straight into Claude, ChatGPT, or any capable model and runs. Start in this order.

1  extraction-prompt.md

The full system prompt with the output schema AND the routing tells inlined, so this one file runs standalone. Fill the context block, paste, run.

2  sample-transcript-and-output.md

A synthetic call and its answer key. Run the prompt against the sample and diff your output against the expected one. A clean pass here means your first real call processes with receipts on day one. The answer key states why each item routes the way it does.

3  routing-rubric.md  +  tier-rules.md

The four routes with their linguistic tells, and the deterministic tier overrides. These are the judgment layer. Adjust the force-review list to your own red lines before the first real call.

4  crm-field-mapping.md

The worksheet from page 5 in editable form. Fill it before anything writes to a live record.
