The method

How It Works

Seven steps. The runnable versions ship as the markdown files in this kit.

01Capture every reply in one queue

The words are the product. Every reply from every sending tool lands in one queue, verbatim. A paraphrased reply has already lost the thing the system exists to collect.

02Clean the text

Strip signatures, footers, disclaimers, and quoted history, so classification reads the human. Most misclassifications are the footer. Keep the raw original alongside for the record.

03Classify with a taxonomy, not a vibe

Six classes with linguistic tells and a numeric confidence. Interested. A question or objection. Not interested. Out of office. Routed away, because people leave companies. And unclassified, because one letter is not evidence, and the schema can say so.

Watch for low confidence. Below 0.8, a person decides the class, and the verdict with its reason becomes the calibration data.

04Honor the exits instantly

The sequence stops the moment words arrive, deterministically, before classification runs. Do-not-contact splits by how the no arrived. Explicit unsubscribe language lands instantly, no model in the path. An inferred no waits for the human verdict, because the irreversible action never rests on an inference. One list, honored at every send path in the guardrails table.

Watch for the tool that keeps sending while a reply sits unread. Nothing burns a domain faster.

magnetiz.ai03

05Re-enroll the out-of-office

An out-of-office is timing, not rejection. The contact pauses until the stated return date plus a bracketed buffer, dates resolved against the day the reply arrived, then resumes where it left off. A delegate named in the auto-reply is a fact for the record, never a new target.

06A person answers anything that matters

Interested replies and real questions alert the channel with the verbatim thread and the lead context alongside. A person writes every answer. Nothing auto-replies, ever.

07Log the words, learn the classes

Supervised first. Every reply is recorded with its class, numeric confidence, the human verdict, and the verdict's reason in one line. Three same-shaped disagreements are a revision candidate, and the objection language is next month's better messaging.

The Six Classes

ClassThe tellWhat happens
Interestednames a time, asks price, real specificsstop · alert · a person replies in the hour
Question / objectiona real question or pushback with contentstop · alert · a person answers same day
Not interesteda clear nostop · explicit unsubscribe lands DNC instantly, an inferred no waits for a person
Out of officeauto-reply, return datepause · re-enroll after the return + buffer
Routed away"I left the company," a named successorrecord updated · never DNC · new contact is a person's call
Unclassifiedtoo little signal to read ("k")always a person · sequence stays paused

Below 0.8 confidence, whatever the class, a person decides. The honesty about what it cannot classify is what makes the confident calls trustworthy.
