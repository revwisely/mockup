The method

How It Works

Seven steps. The runnable versions ship as the markdown files in this kit.

01Capture the claim

Store the submission verbatim, with where the person arrived from. A download alone is not buying intent, and a router that assumes otherwise sells to students and spams recruiters.

02Enrich with evidence

Company, size, role, from public sources, every claim cited. Unknown stays unknown, never guessed. Spend follows use, capped per day.

03Write the account brief

One screen. What we verified, what we could not, with sources. The router and the human reviewer read the same brief, so any decision can be audited in a minute.

04Score fit and intent apart

Enthusiasm cannot fake fit. Fit scores who they are. Intent scores what they did. Kept separate, a crawler visiting twenty-five times is intent without fit, and a perfect-fit visitor on day one is fit without intent. Different numbers, different plays.

Unverified signals score null, never zero. Two or more fit nulls send the sign-up to a person. Intent decays: signals older than ninety days score at half.

Watch for the single combined score. It routes the enthusiastic wrong-fit visitor above the quiet right one.

05Route by band

Five lanes in a stated precedence order, so two implementers get one route. Review first, then qualified, nurture, monitor, no fit. High intent with KNOWN-low fit monitors (the crawler, watched and never sold to). Students, recruiters, and bots get what they asked for, politely, and never a sequence.

magnetiz.ai03

06Drop uncertainty to a person

Review is checked before any band. Fit NULLS, conflicts, borderline scores, and ambiguous duplicates queue for a person with reasons attached. High intent with unknown fit lands here; high intent with known-low fit monitors instead. Duplicates match against existing accounts before anything new is created.

Watch for the router that never asks for help. A queue that is always empty means the rules are guessing.

07Evaluate on the sample sign-ups

Supervised first. Run the four sample sign-ups against the answer key before any real submission. The router earns unattended routing on clean passes, and the no-fit lane earns it last.
