The method

How It Works

Seven steps, read once a quarter. The worksheets for every one of them ship as the markdown files in this kit.

01Name who counts as a champion

A contact on a closed-won account is not a champion. Rate each person on the strongest thing your records show. They signed or approved the budget, ran the rollout, argued for you inside the deal, or only attended. The first three go on the watch list, each with the record that proves it.

Watch for a list built from titles. The person who sat in one demo and the person who signed both look like a past customer contact.

02Set the window and sign the read rule

Pick how long after the start date a move still counts. We start at 90 days and let the readout test it. Before the first message, sign what you compare against, meetings per verified move against meetings per cold account in the same band and weeks, and how sure you need to be. We use a 90% chance.

03Check every alert before it routes

Did they join a different company? Does a second source confirm it, beyond their own profile? Did they start inside the window? Does the new role buy what you sell? A verified move goes on. Not a move is dropped and counted by kind. Cannot tell is held with an owner and never gets a tier.

Watch for the renamed company. When one company acquires another and folds it into its own name, the alert reads your current champion as a new hire somewhere else.

04Let rules written in advance pick the tier

Tier 1 is a verified move by someone who signed or ran the rollout, to a company in your band, in a role that buys. Tier 2 is a weaker champion or an adjacent role. A move to an existing customer goes to its account owner. An AI model can research the move and draft the message, and it never picks who gets one.

05Write from the person and check again at send

About them, the new role and the work you did together. No pitch, no mention of the alert, no link. Right before it sends, check they are still there and read the rendered message. When we read every message before one load, 8 of 129 contacts weren't employees of the company the message was written for.

06Give the account they left an owner task

Every move from a current customer is two events, an opening at the new company and a gap at the old one. The old account's owner finds who now runs the tool or the budget, and meets them before renewal.

07Read it once a quarter against the signed rule

The terms, as this kit uses them

  • Champion. Someone your records show signed, ran the rollout, or argued for you. Not everyone on the account.
  • Verified move. A different company, confirmed at a second source, inside the window.
  • Window. How long after the start date a move still counts.
  • Cold baseline. Your usual outreach to accounts in the same band, over the same weeks.
