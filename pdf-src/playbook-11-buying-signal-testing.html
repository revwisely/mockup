The method

How It Works

Seven steps, four to six weeks per test. The worksheets for every one of them ship as the markdown files in this kit.

01Shortlist two or three signals

Few signals, each one visible from outside the account. Rate every candidate on whether you can see it before the conversation, how many teams already act on it, and what checking it costs. In the 2026 State of B2B GTM report most teams act on content engagement (52%), website visits (42%) and product usage (40%). Lean toward at least one signal fewer teams use.

Watch for signals you can only see on the call. Those are discovery questions and stay off the list.

02Write the fact that must still be true

One card per signal. The event you see, the one fact that would make it meaningless if it stopped being true, where to check that fact, how long the signal stays fresh, the opening line it makes possible, and the rule for when it retires.

03Split the list and sign the rule

The signal arm gets accounts that show the signal. The comparison arm gets accounts from the same band that do not, with your usual message. Keep the sender, channel, days and follow-ups the same. Aim for 100 accounts per arm and about 10 replies across both before reading.

Watch for a test too small to read. Below 60 accounts per arm it tells you nothing, and calling it early is how a lucky week becomes a strategy.

04Check every fact before every batch

Check each account's fact at its source. Send only when it is still true and inside its fresh window, and hold the account when the answer is "cannot tell". Then read the rendered message the tool will actually send. When we read every message before one load, 8 of 129 contacts had already moved to other companies.

05Send both arms and count

Replies per account contacted and meetings booked, for each arm. Keep the pre-send tallies too, because they show how often the signal goes stale or cannot be checked.

06Read it against the signed rule

At 100 accounts per arm and the low reply rates of most cold outreach, a gap under about three times is usually noise. 9% against 3% is a strong enough hint to act on. 6% against 3% is too close to call. Report the result as directional.

On the read date the log showsDecision
The gap that counts, minimum count metKeep. Scale it, keep the check
A gap, but something else could explain itHold. One more period
Too close to callFix. One message change, one more period
No gap, or the card's retirement rule hitRetire
Below the minimum countKeep recording. Decide nothing yet

07Scale what earned its place, test the next

A kept signal scales with the pre-send check still on every batch and gets read again in a quarter, because a signal that works today can stop working once more teams act on it. A retired signal makes room for the next one on the shortlist.

The terms, as this kit uses them

  • Signal. An event you can see from outside the account that might mean it is ready to buy.
  • The fact. What must still be true for the signal to mean anything.
  • Fresh window. How long after the event the signal still counts.
  • Comparison arm. Accounts from the same band without the signal, sent your usual message.
  • Minimum count. Below it the read is too thin, and nothing is decided.
