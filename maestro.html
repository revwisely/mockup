// The Missing Piece

The missing piece is
architecture.

Most companies experimenting with agents deploy tools, test workflows, and automate small tasks. Nothing connects. Without an architecture, agents stay isolated experiments.

Pilot to production is the single largest problem this category gets hired to solve, at 16% of stated problems across 36 surveyed providers.

Maestro is an AI-enabled revenue architecture. It defines how data moves across systems, how signals get detected, how agents analyze activity, how insights get generated, and how teams execute against them.

It creates one operational layer across marketing, sales, and operations, so revenue stops depending on individual heroics and starts running as a designed system.

// How It Works

Three layers,
connected.

Signals come in, decisions get made, work gets executed, and every step is logged.

maestro_pipeline/ — magnetiz
1
01_Signal_Layer.py </>

The system watches for change across your accounts and market. CRM activity, product usage, pipeline movement, marketing engagement, hiring, leadership changes, funding, and what the companies you sell to say publicly.

2
02_Intelligence_Layer.py </>

Signals get enriched and scored against a 100-point model covering title fit, company fit, signal strength, and engagement. Scoring produces a routing decision with an owner attached instead of another dashboard.

3
03_Execution_Layer.py </>

Work gets routed, drafted, and written back to the system of record. Account summaries, prioritized queues, outreach drafts, CRM updates. Humans approve anything carrying brand or revenue risk.

4
04_Eval_Gate.py </>

Every output is scored against binary pass or fail tests before it ships. Gating thresholds stop a failing run before it reaches a customer.
